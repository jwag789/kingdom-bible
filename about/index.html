Bible stories for kids

Stories they can touch.

Kingdom Bible tells the great stories of the Bible in cut-paper pictures that move when little fingers tap them, read aloud with music, and each one has a game at the end.

Free. No ads, no accounts, nothing to sign up for.

What’s inside

A picture book that plays back

Every story is told a few sentences at a time, over a painting that comes alive. Children tap, swipe and listen at their own pace.

Pictures you can touch

Tap the picture and things happen: Jesus stops under the tree, the sea parts, the stars come out. Every page has little surprises to find.

Read aloud, with music

A warm narrator reads every page while the words light up, with gentle music and sounds made for each scene. Pre-readers can follow along on their own.

A game for every story

Bring the animals onto the ark two by two, fill twelve baskets of leftovers, march round Jericho. Each story has its own little game.

Stickers and a kingdom to build

Stickers are hidden in the pictures, lost lambs wait to be found, and every finished story adds people, animals and places to your child’s own paper kingdom.

For grown-ups

Made for reading together

  • Read it in your own voiceRecord a story page by page, and the app reads it back in your voice instead of the narrator’s. Send it to grandparents as a link and they can record one too.
  • Real Bible verses, one tap awayEvery part of a story links to the passage it comes from, in the Berean Standard Bible, so you can read the real words together.
  • Questions to talk aboutEach story ends with a few questions and a memory verse, ready for the car, the dinner table or bedtime.
  • Bedtime modeSofter, dimmer pictures and calmer music for winding down at night.
  • Safe by designNo ads, no accounts, no tracking. Progress is saved on your device, and grown-up features sit behind a quick “ask a grown-up” check.
  • Works offlineOnce a story has been opened, it keeps working without a connection, on a plane or a long drive.

Questions

Good to know

Who is it for?

Young children reading with a grown-up, early readers on their own, and anyone new to the Bible who wants its big stories told simply and warmly.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every story and game is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. The tip jar is entirely optional.

What information does it collect?

None. There are no accounts and no analytics. Stickers, progress and your recordings stay on your device. A recording only leaves it if you choose to send it to family, and then only people with the link can hear it.

Which Bible does it use?

The stories are retold in simple words for children. The verses behind each part come from the Berean Standard Bible, a modern translation in the public domain.

Will there be more stories?

Yes. New stories are added to the shelves as they’re made. Tips help make them happen sooner.
